Roadmap & Milestone

The milestone of PLANA is a series of challenges to shift paradigm of aerial transportation

2021

– PLANA Establishment (July 12nd)
– Pre-seed: Investment Attraction

2022

– Seed: Investment Attraction
– Completion of CP-01 Conceptual Design
– R&D Center Establishment
– Selected as a Baby-Unicorn Startup by Ministry of SMEs and Startups (ROK)
– LOI with Ghenus Air for 20 Aircraft Purchase
– MOU with EP System for Battery Technologies
– MOU with HANSEO University for Flight Test
– MOU with Seoyon E-Hwa for Cabin Design

– Rollout of Subscale Prototype Aircraft and Validation
– Winner of iF Design Award 2023 (Professional Concept)
