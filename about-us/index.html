Roadmap & Milestone

The milestone of PLANA is a series of challenges to shift paradigm of aerial transportation

2021

– PLANA founded (July 12)
– Pre-seed investment round

2022

– Completion of CP-01 conceptual design
– R&D center established
– Seed investment round
– Selected as a Baby-Unicorn startup by the Ministry of SMEs and Startups (ROK)
– LOI with Ghenus Air for 20 aircraft
– Partner MOUs for battery technology (EP System), flight test (Hanseo University) and cabin design (Seoyon E-Hwa)

2023

– Subscale prototype aircraft rolled out and validated
– Winner of the iF Design Award 2023 (Professional Concept)
– Startup Award, Korean Mobility Innovation Awards
– USA branch office established
– LOIs with SkyTaxi (50 aircraft) and Hi-Air (30 aircraft)
– MOUs with 10 industry and academic partners

2024

– Improved propeller system and updated conceptual design
– First subscale UAV prototype sold
– Bridge investment round, including US investors
– Awarded a Korean Government ‘Super Gap 1000+ Project’ grant
– Selected by Greater Seattle Partners for SelectUSA
– LOI with M.I. Air for 7 aircraft

2025

– PLANA US, Inc. established and headquarters relocated to the USA
– Full-scale hybrid power system development
– Pre-A investment round

2026

– Iron Bird: hybrid-electric power system proof of concept with off-the-shelf components (Q2)
– US headquarters in Orange County, California

2027

– Critical design complete for the full-scale hybrid powertrain (Q3)
– Series A investment round

2028

– FAA Certification Basis (G-1) (Q1)
– Full-scale mockup with interior (Q2)
– Full-scale unmanned flying platform (Q2)
– Series B investment round

2029

– FAA Means of Compliance (G-2) (Q1)
– Full-scale aircraft rollout (Q2)
– First flight of the full-scale demonstrator (FSD) (Q4)

2030

– IPO

2031

– Final review: FTA and DO-178C (Q3)

2032

– Type Certificate (TC) under FAA Part 21.17(b) (Q2)
– Go-to-market: commercial and special-mission operations
